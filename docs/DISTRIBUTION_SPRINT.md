# Distribution sprint

Current priority: turn public technical evidence into reproducible downstream use.

## Wedge
The primary adoption surface is the FLOP Conformance Lab. Router and Control Center remain adjacent tools discovered through it; they are not the lead message.

## KPIs
Track, in order:
1. targeted proposals delivered
2. external profile runs
3. downstream pinned integrations
4. VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_CI consumers
5. upstream references to retained evidence

Stars, forks, clones and maintainer self-tests are visibility signals, not adoption.

## Feature gate
Until at least one external pinned integration exists, new general-purpose features are deprioritized unless they unblock an identified external adopter, reproduce a new upstream resolution/regression, close a safety/correctness defect, or materially reduce the five-minute integration path.

## Target
First milestone: 1 VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_CI consumer. Next: 3 independent consumers, then one recurring upstream/downstream dependency or reference.
